## The basics
- **Length:** ~75 s (inside the 50 s – 1:30 range).
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps, high-bitrate H.264 (source clips are 1080p60 at ~110 Mbps, so this keeps full quality).

- **Story:** one day on the Tides ocean, dawn → storm → building → underwater → night → sunset → "join us".
  The edit breathes like a tide: slow swells in, slow swells out.

## The video, second by second

## Look & feel
- **Grade:** one look on every clip: deep teal water, warm golden highlights, lifted blacks, soft bloom, fine film grain, gentle vignette, thin letterbox bars (2.39:1) during the story, opening to full frame for the info card.
- **Motion:** every shot gets a slow push-in or drift (no static frames); gentle speed ramps (100% → 50%) into key moments, using the 60 fps footage.
- **Transitions (calm only):** water ripple dissolves, light-leak crossfades, "surface break" warp into the underwater part, depth-blur pulls, dip-to-white on the title. No hard whips or glitch.
- **Text:** one elegant serif for titles + one clean sans for captions; small, centred, fades with a slight upward drift and letter-spacing breathe. Never more than two lines on screen at once.

## Sound
- **Music:** calm, royalty-free track (I will shortlist three; see question below).
- **Ambience under it:** ocean waves throughout, rain in the storm part, muffled underwater sound + bubbles during the dive, crackle at the campfire.
- Soft whooshes / water swishes on the transitions, kept very quiet.

## Tools / repos and what each does
| Tool | Used for |
|---|---|
| **ffmpeg** | cutting, slow-mo, colour grade, grain, letterbox, final encode, watermark |
| **hyperframes** (HeyGen, Apache-2.0) | all animated text, title reveal, captions and the info card, rendered as HTML → transparent video and laid over the footage |
| **earshot** | finds and forges the ambience and transition sounds locally, and critiques the final audio mix |
| **lemo-opuscar** | film-style recipes (grain, bloom, light leaks) used as a reference for the look |
| **taste-skill / impeccable** | typography and layout rules for the titles, info card and thumbnail |
| **rembg** | cutting the player out of a frame for the thumbnail |
| **playwright-cli** | renders the thumbnail from HTML at full resolution |
| **Python (Pillow / numpy)** | grade checks, watermark pattern, frame QA |
| **Google Workspace skill** | uploading to Google Drive |
| Not used | **onetake** (non-commercial licence — not allowed for paid client work), the ArtCraft apps (filmcraft, effectcraft, etc. — desktop apps that would need full Rust builds, no gain over the above), open-higgsfield (paid API), VoiceStudio (no voice-over planned) |

Everything runs locally: no API calls, no fees.
