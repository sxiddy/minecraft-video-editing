# Notes C: pro Minecraft server trailers, studied frame by frame

Sources: full_1fps / first20 sheets, plus 12 fps strips I pulled (`<id>/strip_*.jpg`) across the text and logo animations. Cut data comes from metrics_all.json. Sizes are % of frame height (FH). Frame counts are at 24/30 fps. Each strip covers 1.33 s (16 frames at 12 fps), so timings are ±1 frame at 12 fps.

## Cross-video patterns (what the best share)
1. **Calm open, accelerating middle, flash into the logo.** Hypixel is the model. It spends 25 s on slow dissolves, then the cuts compress to 0.75 s, then a white flash (2 frames) reveals the logo. Even calm trailers keep energy by **tightening shot length over time** (Aero 3 s → 1 s, locked to 2 beats).
2. **Text is SMALL and sparse.** Hypixel cards ~2.2% FH, Letal titles ~2.5% FH, IP lines 1–2% FH. Only gameplay callouts (TubNet ~5%) and captions (~4.5%) are larger. Logos sit at 9–17% FH (shield/crest up to ~45%).
3. **Pixel/Minecraft-flavour display font + clean sans for info.** Hypixel and TubNet use a pixel display for headlines and a plain sans (Poppins-like) for URL/date. Calm or whimsical servers use a script (Fairy) or a rounded sans (Bannerfall).
4. **Wide tracking, ALL CAPS, warm off-white** (#F2E6C8) on black or over footage with a soft shadow, no glow. This reads as the most "premium" (Hypixel, Letal).
5. **Text animation timings:**
   - Fade-in 3–6 frames at 24 fps (Hypixel ~6f, TubNet ~10f with a 5–10 px rise).
   - Typewriter at ~2 frames per letter (Letal), each letter blur-fading in.
   - Logo write-on ~1 s per hero glyph, ~3 s total (Fairy).
   - Logo scale settle 102% → 100% over ~4–6 frames after the white flash (Hypixel).
   - Out: blur + fade 6–8 frames, or simply cut out with the shot.
   - Holds: feature cards 1.5–2 s, logo 4–8 s, end card 3–9 s.
6. **Title cards double as rhythmic rests.** Black frame, a two-word ALL-CAPS line ("NEW MAP", "NEW BOSSES", "GET OUT ALIVE") on a bar downbeat, ~1.5 s.
7. **The IP or URL always sits under the logo** in a small sans (TubNet, Hypixel, Letal). Bannerfall and Aero omit it. Do not copy that.
8. **Real game UI as proof.** Item tooltips (Hypixel) and nametags or holograms (TubNet) shown full frame sell features honestly.
9. **Every shot moves.** Slow drone push, orbit or crane. No static gameplay shots except Fairy's locked forest.

## For a CALM, dreamy server
- Borrow Fairy's **firefly/particle hand-drawn logo write-on**, Hypixel's **dissolve-led first 20 s plus small wide-tracked cards**, and Aero's **2-beat cut grid** for the middle so energy rises without aggression.
- Keep a 2-frame soft white (or pastel) flash for the logo hit.
- Show the IP plus a date in a clean sans at ~2% FH under the logo. Hold 5–6 s.
- Avoid Fairy's 20 s of near-black with no cuts before anything happens. Put a striking image in the first 1 s.
