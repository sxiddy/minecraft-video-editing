Method: I read the full_1fps sheets for all 7 videos, plus 12 fps full-res strips across key text and transition moments (`<id>/strip_*.jpg`). Cut data comes from metrics_all.json. Sizes are estimated from 1280x720 frames (% = cap height / frame height, written H). Frame counts are at 24/25 fps.

Caveat: the cut detector logs cross-dissolves and flashes as 2 cuts 0.02-0.05 s apart. These are soft transitions, not two hard cuts.

# Cross-video patterns
1. Cut rate: Vexel and Horizon use median shots of 2-3.3 s (cinematic), plus one short flurry of 0.2-0.4 s shots for energy. Only Horizon's ArcaneMC lands more than 50% of cuts on the beat. Feature beats last ~4 s (one per 2 bars).
2. Hook: the best ones show a logo or beauty shot within 0.5-3.5 s. Long black openings are weak (SodeMC 7 s, ArcaneMC 3 s). Cinematic rig openers (Royce, PvP HQ) feel premium but slow.
3. Transitions: 55-75% hard cuts. The rest are 1-3 white flashes at the big moments, 1-frame zoom/motion-blur whips, and dissolves. The standout trick is text as the transition (the fly-through "PRESENTS").
4. Typography: every pro trailer uses exactly 2 tiers.
   - Title: caps sans, Bold/Black or condensed, wide tracking (0.15-0.35 em), 3-6.5% H; white with glow, or a gold gradient.
   - Sub: same family, 1.3-2.2% H, caps, tracked, white at 70-100%.
   - Position: centre, or centred in the lower third (75-80% down). Never at the top.
   - Effects: soft glow or soft drop shadow only. No hard strokes, except on the logo itself.
5. Text animation numbers:
   - In: 3-12 frames (3-frame scale-pop with overshoot; 10-12 frame random-letter decode; typewriter at ~1 letter per frame).
   - The sub lags the title by 4-6 frames.
   - Hold: 2.5-4 s (rarely under 1 s), with a slow 2-3% scale drift.
   - Out: 3-12 frames (3-frame motion-blur smear or 12-frame fade).
6. Logo: a 3D or extruded logo at 15-35% H, revealed by a flash, blur-in or fly-in and held 2-4 s. It comes back at the end.
7. Game UI: big real GUIs and tooltips (40-50% H) prove the features; characters carry nametags.
8. End card: logo plus IP (plus store and Discord). The IP is small (1.5-2.5% H) but held 3-8 s. The best ones show the IP as a plain lowercase domain. Two (PvP HQ, Royce) show no IP at all, which is an easy gap to beat.
9. Grade: shaders, bloom, teal/orange or moody coloured light; vignette.
