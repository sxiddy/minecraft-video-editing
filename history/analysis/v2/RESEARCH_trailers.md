Method: YouTube storyboards (1 frame/s) for 21 trailers; 13 studied sheet by sheet so far.

Limitation: 1 frame/s shows type, layout and structure, not cut speed or transition motion.

## Findings
1. Feature captions: uppercase geometric sans (Montserrat / Gotham / Bebas family), centred, small,
   wide tracking, with a tiny subline under it. KiZaa: warm gold main line + white sub.
   Horizon: very wide tracking ("C U S T O M  W O R L D"). No serif, no italic script, nowhere.
2. Every trailer reveals the server's own logo (3D or illustrated): ArcaneMC, VaroxCraft, RoyceMC.
3. End card: logo + IP in one small clean line, or the Minecraft pixel font on black (Hypixel "PLAYTEST
   OUT NOW", Nuvguard "ip: mc.nuvguard.com").
4. In-game UI used as graphics: inventories, crafting GUI, rank-up pop-ups, nametags (Vexel, Kelvin).
5. Letterbox in almost all of them (Kelvin, KiZaa, Horizon).
6. Casual SMPs (Bannerfall) use a rounded bold lowercase sans with coloured keywords.

## STYLE GUIDE v3 (synthesised from notes_A/B/C: 21 trailers + RuneSpire bar)
- Cuts: ~80% hard cuts ON the beat. Shots 1-2 s (2-4 beats), one 0.2-0.4 s flurry burst, max 1-2 flashes, 1 signature warp, dips to black for section breaks. No transition soup.
- Hook: striking image in frame 1; logo legible by 3 s; white flash + 102->100% settle on the logo hit.
- Type tiers: logo 8-15% FH pixel banded+outline; title 4-6% FH pixel caps wide tracking; sub 1.5-2.2% FH; IP 2% FH lowercase. Centre or lower third, never top. Soft shadow only (outline only on logo/pops).
- Text in 3-12 f (decode random-order letters ~10 f, or 3 f brightness pop + shimmer); sub lags 4-6 f; hold 1.5-2.5 s with 2-3% drift; out on hard cut or 6 f blur-fade.
- Diegetic game UI = strongest device: gold pixel pop ("ISLAND DISCOVERED!") with sparkles ~9% FH, advancement toasts, multiplayer screen.
- End card: logo + CTA + IP decoded per letter, hold 3-4 s, fade 1 s.
- Grade: saturated warm/teal, bloom, vignette; never grey or oversaturated-cheap.
